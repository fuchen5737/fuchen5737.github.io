Before you order.

Short answers. No trip to the yard required.

How deep should topsoil be?

About 2 inches for topdressing a lawn, 6–8 inches for a raised bed, and 4–6 inches for a new garden bed. The calculator defaults to 2 inches.

How much does a cubic yard of topsoil cover?

About 324 sq ft at 1 inch, 162 sq ft at 2 inches, and 108 sq ft at 3 inches.

Should I add extra for compaction?

Yes. Soil settles and compacts. Add 10–15% for raised beds and new beds.

Can I mix topsoil with compost?

Yes. Many gardeners use a mix of topsoil and compost. Enter the total depth you need, then split the materials by volume.

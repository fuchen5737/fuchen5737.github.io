Before you order.

Short answers. No trip to the yard required.

How many square feet are on a sod pallet?

Most sod pallets cover about 500 sq ft, but the number varies by supplier and grass type. Check the pallet label.

How much extra sod should I order?

Order 5–10% extra for cuts, edges, and small mistakes. Sod cannot stretch.

Does sod need topsoil?

A firm, prepared base matters more than a thick layer of topsoil. Loosen the top few inches, level it, and keep it moist before installation.

Can I install sod myself?

Yes for small areas, but prep and watering are the real work. For a large lawn, compare the cost of delivery and installation.

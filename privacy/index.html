Privacy

Plain English, no fog.

The tools run in your browser. Your inputs stay there, unless you choose to allow analytics.

Your inputs stay in the browser

Names, move dates, car mileage, home profiles, garden dimensions, camping choices, and checked items are saved in your browser’s local storage so a result survives a refresh. The tools do not send those inputs to a server. Clearing browser data or switching devices removes them.

No accounts or uploads

There is no signup, login, email capture, VIN entry, address entry, or file upload. Copy and print actions use your browser’s clipboard and print dialog.

Hosting logs

GitHub Pages, which serves this site, may keep standard server logs such as IP address, user agent, and requested URL for security and reliability. Those logs are separate from the tools and are not used to rebuild your inputs.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics 4 (Measurement ID G-QT91VKFN8L) to count visits and learn which tools are useful. The Google tag runs in consent mode on every page. Before you choose, it does not send a pageview or set analytics cookies. If you allow it, Google may set cookies and process visit data as described in Google’s privacy policy. If you decline, analytics storage stays off.

Affiliate links

Some pages may link to products or services. If a link earns a commission, that does not change the price you pay. The tools are free regardless, and affiliate links do not affect the calculations or generated results.

Guidance, not a professional service

Car and home maintenance pages are general planners, not inspections, warranties, or professional advice. Wedding, pet, and camping tools are organizers, not a substitute for local rules, safety advice, or a licensed professional.

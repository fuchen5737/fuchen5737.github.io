Privacy, in plain English.

Tag the Vows is a browser tool, not a data business. Here is exactly what happens to the names you type.

Your names stay in the tab

The hashtag generator runs as JavaScript in your browser. The names, date, and story you enter are used to build the tags on your device. This page does not send them to a server.

No accounts, no uploads

There is no signup, no login, no file upload, and no email capture. The “Save image” button draws a PNG in your browser and downloads it directly to your device.

Hosting logs

Like any website, the static host that serves these pages may keep standard server logs (IP address, user agent, requested URL) for security and reliability. Those logs are separate from the generator and are not used to rebuild your names.

No analytics scripts

This build does not include third-party analytics, advertising pixels, or social embeds. If that changes, this page will say so before the change ships.

Questions

Questions about this policy? Write to hello@tagthevows.com.

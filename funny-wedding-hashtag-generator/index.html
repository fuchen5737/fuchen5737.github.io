    Good questions.

    Short answers, no wedding-planning fluff.

    What makes a wedding hashtag funny without being cringe?

    Specific beats generic. A tag about your dog, your first date, or a running joke will land harder than a generic pun. Keep it short enough to spell after two drinks.

    Are funny wedding hashtags tacky?

    Only if they punch down or make guests feel like they are outside the joke. A playful tag that fits the couple is a conversation starter, not a costume.

    Can we mix funny and classic tags?

    Yes. Use a classic tag on the invitation and a funnier one on the reception signs, the photo booth, or the group chat.

    How do we make a funny tag with our last name?

    Try a playful plural, a mock takeover, or a phrase that sounds like a headline: The Smith Takeover, Happily Ever Smith, Bye Bye Smith. The last-name page has more formulas.

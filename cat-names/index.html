Three rules for a name that sticks.

A cat learns the sound before the spelling. Pick something you will be happy to call across a quiet house.

01

Say it, then call it

Two clear syllables beat a clever spelling. Try it in a normal voice and in your best treat voice.

02

Test the nickname

Every name becomes a nickname. If you cannot stand the short version, pick a different name.

03

Keep it distinct

Avoid a name that sounds like no, sit, or the name of another pet in the house.

Good questions.

Short answers, no cat-show politics.

Is Cat Name Club free?

Yes. No account, no email, and no paid unlock. Pull as many boards as you like.

How many names do I get?

Each run pulls 24 names. Tap the star to keep the good ones in your shortlist.

Do favorites save?

Yes. Your shortlist is saved in this browser. Clearing browser data will clear it.

Are the names hand-picked?

The list is hand-built. The board shuffles and filters it; it does not invent names from random letters.

Can I use a name for a dog?

The list is written for cats, but many names work for any pet. The filter is a starting point, not a rule.

Is the warrior generator official?

No. It is a fan-made prefix and suffix tool. It is not affiliated with the Warriors book series and uses no official art or logos.

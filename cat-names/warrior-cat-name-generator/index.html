Good questions.

Short answers, no cat-show politics.

What is a warrior cat name generator?

A fan-made tool that combines a nature prefix (Thunder, River, Shadow) with a suffix (claw, heart, fur) to create names in the style of the Warriors books.

Is this affiliated with the Warriors series?

No. It is an unofficial fan tool. It uses no official art, logos, or character names from the books.

Can I use these names in fan fiction?

Many fans do. Check the series publisher's fan-work policy before publishing or selling anything, and do not use official artwork.

How are the names generated?

From a hand-made list of 40 prefixes and 41 suffixes. The generator shuffles them and avoids duplicates in one scroll.

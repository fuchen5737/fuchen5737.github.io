Three rules for a name that sticks.

A dog learns the sound before the meaning. Pick something you will be happy to yell across a park.

01

Say it out loud

Two clear syllables beat a clever spelling. Try it in a normal voice and in your loudest park voice.

02

Keep the commands clear

Avoid names that sound like sit, stay, down, or no. The dog should not hear a command every time you call.

03

Test the nickname

Every name becomes a nickname. If you cannot stand the short version, pick a different name.

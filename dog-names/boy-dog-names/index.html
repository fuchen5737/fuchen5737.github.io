Good questions.

Short answers, no puppy-training lecture.

What makes a good boy dog name?

A clear sound, a nickname you like, and a name that does not sound like a command. Short and strong is a reliable start.

Are these names only for male dogs?

The list is filtered to boy and any-gender names, but dogs do not care about gender. Use what fits.

Can I mix a boy name with a cute vibe?

Yes. The vibe filter is separate from the gender filter, so you can pull cute boy names, strong boy names, and everything in between.

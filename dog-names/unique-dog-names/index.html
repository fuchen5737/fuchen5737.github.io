Good questions.

Short answers, no puppy-training lecture.

What counts as a unique dog name?

A name that is not already on every park list: a myth, a place, a foreign name, or a word with a story. The unique filter favors those.

Will a unique name be hard for a dog to learn?

Not if it is short and clear. Unusual does not have to mean complicated; test it out loud before you commit.

Can I still use a common name?

Of course. The unique filter is a starting point, not a rule. Use the star to keep whatever fits.

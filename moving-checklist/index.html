Before you pack.

Short answers. No moving-day fluff.

Is Last Box really free?

Yes. No account, no email, and no paid unlock. Build as many plans as you need.

Where is my progress saved?

In this browser, using local storage. Clearing your browser data or switching devices will clear the checks.

Can I print the checklist?

Yes. Build a plan, then use the Print button. The printed sheet hides the site navigation and keeps the dated tasks.

Does it work for a cross-country move?

Yes. Choose cross-country and the plan adds travel, car registration, mail forwarding, and a first-night plan.

My move is in two weeks. Is it too late?

No. Start with the one-week and move-day phases; past-due items are marked so you can see what to do first.

Do I need a moving company?

No. The plan works for movers, a rental truck, or a van full of friends. The tasks stay the same.

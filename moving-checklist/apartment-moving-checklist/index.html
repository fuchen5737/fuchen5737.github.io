The building is the part people forget. Put these on the calendar before the boxes.

Book the elevator

Buildings need notice. Reserve a window, measure the door, and confirm the truck can stop outside.

02

Photograph the empty unit

03

Know the handover

Keys, fobs, parking passes, mailbox keys, and the building's move-out checklist.

Before you pack.

Short answers. No moving-day fluff.

Does the plan include the elevator?

Yes. The apartment plan adds an elevator or parking reservation task, plus a handover checklist for keys, passes, and the mailbox.

What if my building has no elevator?

Skip the reservation task and use the extra time to plan the stairs, the truck spot, and a doorstop.

Can I use it for a lease-end move?

Yes. The plan includes a final walkthrough, meter photos, and a deposit-friendly clean-out reminder.

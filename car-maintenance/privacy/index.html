Privacy, in plain English.

Milemark is a planner in your browser. No account, no VIN, no service records on a server.

Your profile stays in the browser

The odometer, annual mileage, driving profile, powertrain, and checked services are saved in this browser’s local storage. They are not sent to a server by the planner. Clearing browser data or switching devices will clear the saved log.

No accounts, no uploads

There is no signup, no login, no VIN entry, no file upload, and no email capture. The copy and print buttons use your browser’s clipboard and print dialog.

Hosting logs

Like any website, the static host that serves these pages may keep standard server logs (IP address, user agent, requested URL) for security and reliability.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics 4 (Measurement ID G-5QWJZ0635S) to count visits and learn which pages and tools are useful. The Google tag runs in consent mode on every page. Before you choose, it does not send a pageview or set analytics cookies. If you allow it, Google may set cookies and process visit data as described in Google’s privacy policy. If you decline, analytics storage stays off.

General guidance

Maintenance intervals on this site are general estimates. They are not a manufacturer schedule, warranty statement, or safety inspection. Follow your owner’s manual and have safety concerns inspected by a qualified mechanic.

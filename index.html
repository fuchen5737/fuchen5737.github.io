Good questions.

Short answers before you pick a tool.

Is everything free?

Yes. The tools are free and do not require an account. Some pages may eventually include affiliate links; the tool itself stays usable without buying anything.

Do you save my inputs?

Only in your browser’s local storage, so a checklist or shortlist survives a refresh. The site does not send your inputs to a server. Clearing browser data removes them.

Why are these on one site?

They share the same idea: take a small, recurring job and turn it into a usable action instead of another article. The tools keep their own identities, and this page is the index.

How is the site tracked?

We use consent-gated Google Analytics to count visits and see which tools are used. Before you allow analytics, no pageview or analytics cookie is set. See the privacy page.

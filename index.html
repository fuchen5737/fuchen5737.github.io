    Good questions.

    Short answers, no wedding-planning fluff.

    Is the wedding hashtag generator really free?

    Yes. There is no signup, no email wall, and no paid unlock. Print as many sheets as you like.

    Do you store our names?

    No. The generator runs in your browser. Your names, date, and story are not sent to a server by this page.

    How many hashtags should we use?

    Pick one or two as the official tag and keep the rest for the group chat. A single tag is easier for guests to remember and type.

    Can we use these on Instagram?

    Yes. Before you commit, search the exact tag on Instagram to make sure another couple is not already using it.

    What if we do not want to use a last name?

    Leave the last-name field blank. The generator will lean on your first names, initials, date, and story instead.

    Can we print the proof sheet?

    Yes. Use your browser’s print command on the proof sheet; the navigation and form are hidden in print styles.
